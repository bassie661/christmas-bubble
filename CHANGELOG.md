# Christmas Bubble Cards release notes

## v1.0.1

- Each button supports `christmas_trees` (0–36) and `christmas_houses` (0–8) in YAML. Set either count to zero to hide it.
- Pines of different heights form groves in the foreground and background. In gusts, trunks and lower branches sway gently while the treetops bend freely.
- The largest Alpine chalet sits in the center, flanked by smaller chalets with varied roofs, timber finishes, balconies and window light. Even in dense groves, the facades stay visible. A small pine may stand at a front corner.
- Chimney smoke rises diagonally in a light breeze. Even in the strongest gusts, the plume stays short and full near the chimney and breaks into separate wisps at its downwind tip.
